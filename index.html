A gentle look at IVF

Preparing the eggs

Medication helps several eggs mature while your response is carefully monitored.

Fertilization

Egg and sperm are brought together in the laboratory using the approach best suited to your case.

Embryo development

Early embryos are observed for a few days as they grow in carefully controlled conditions.

Transfer

A selected embryo can be placed gently into the uterus at the appropriate time.

Preserving possibilities

Suitable embryos may be frozen for a future transfer when clinically appropriate.
